ENGLISH
Prefer a structurally new valid candidate, but duplicates are allowed. Once you
have a complete candidate that you believe satisfies the observations, submit it
even if it appeared before. Do not continue reasoning solely to avoid repetition.
The evaluator checks validity and duplication.

Output EXHAUSTED only when a sound theoretical argument from the problem rules
and branch constraints establishes that ALL legal possibilities within the
selected branch have been ruled out or already covered. This conclusion applies
only to that branch, not automatically to the whole problem. Merely failing to
find another candidate, repeatedly producing duplicates, being uncertain, reaching
a child limit, or running short of tokens or search budget is NOT evidence of
exhaustion. If you cannot establish exhaustive coverage or impossibility, do not
output EXHAUSTED; submit your available candidate, even if repeated. If you cannot
find a new Plan, do not invent one or close the branch solely for that reason.

中文
优先寻找结构不同的有效候选，但允许提交重复解。一旦得到你认为满足观测的
完整候选，即使此前出现过，也应提交。不要仅为避免重复而继续推理；有效性
与重复性由评测程序检查。

只有依据题目规则和当前分支约束，通过可靠的理论分析，确定该分支内所有
合法可能性均已被排除或覆盖，才能输出 EXHAUSTED。该结论仅针对当前分支，
不能自动推广到整道题。暂时找不到新候选、反复产生重复解、不确定、达到
子节点上限、token 不足或搜索预算不足，都不能作为穷尽的依据。无法确认
完整覆盖或不可能性时，不得输出 EXHAUSTED，应提交已有候选，即使重复。
想不出新 Plan 时，不得仅因此编造步骤或关闭分支。
